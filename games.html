Games

Hammered

A wizard on probation for Flying Under the Influence, stripped of magic except a sledgehammer and a freeze-tool frame, guarding a dungeon cell from waves of monsters. Built for GeorgiaDev Winter Jam 2025 with a five-person team — I helped bring the sledgehammer-swinging chaos to life in Unity. Fair warning: Firefox does not like this game, and neither do the monsters.

Spotlight Showdown

A rhythm fighting game where masked performers battle for the crowd's favor, built around "Beat Data" instead of standard frame data — every strike is timed to a 150 BPM track. My team took 1st place with it at the GSU / State of Georgia Global Game Jam 2026. Requires two players (or one very understanding roommate).

Carve

A sandbox skiing prototype set at Yeti's Lawn Ski Resort, built over 2.5 months as a senior capstone project. Explore three slopes, collect stickers, talk to NPCs, and hit some genuinely sick jumps. I built the environment models (and, memorably, the hotdog physics).

Club Wyld

A rhythm-based DJ simulator built in one week for Major Jam 7: Wild with Sophia R. Your brother, Lil Dump Truck, has bailed on hosting duties, so it's up to you to run the DJ booth for Club Wyld — swap mixtapes, work the crowd, and try not to just spam the soundboard for laughs (no judgment if you do).

Lorenzo's Looting

A goblin heist puzzle-platformer made for GMTK Game Jam 2021. Lorenzo has spotted the shiniest object of his life — at the top of a castle. Navigate the dungeons below by strategically depositing and withdrawing coins at ATMs to manage your weight, since you can't make the jump carrying it all.

Gluppo!

A small, deliberately unhinged creature sim made for a Game Studies class. Walk, eat, and sleep as Gluppo — that's it, that's the game, and it might be a little overkill for what it is. If you want to play again, just reload the page. I got lazy.

The Virtual Museum of Natural Wonders

A virtual walkthrough of an artificial future. Visitors travel through time and across all seven continents to explore humanity's growing integration with AI and technology, with each wing telling its own story of how a region's culture, landscape, and economy shaped — and was reshaped by — that evolution.
