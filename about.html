About Me

Welcome to my portfolio! My name is Nic, and I'm a rising senior studying Computational Media at Georgia Tech — which is a fancy way of saying I never quite picked a lane between film, games, and 3D art, so I just do all three.

Most recently, that's meant writing and world-building a ten-episode animated series (Punks for Hire), leading a 40-person team through a semester of game development with GT's Video Game Development Club, and taking home 1st place at the GSU / State of Georgia Global Game Jam with a rhythm fighting game called Spotlight Showdown. I'm currently modeling a 3D environmental installation for a grant project with artist Belinda Person, and this past summer I spent my days teaching kids Blender, Adobe Animate, and Lua at iD Tech — which is a great way to get very fast at explaining complicated software to people who have never opened it before.

Outside of the deadlines, I'm chasing whatever new tool or technique catches my attention that week, keeping tabs on the games industry, and generally looking for an excuse to make something. My goal is simple: end up somewhere in the entertainment industry, making things people actually feel something about.
